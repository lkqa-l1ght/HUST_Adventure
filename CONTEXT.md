# Hust Adventure

A roguelite survival RPG where a HUST university student battles programming bugs and academic challenges across campus.

## Language

### Protagonist & Resources

**Student**:
The player-controlled university student fighting for academic survival across campus.
_Avoid_: Hero, Player Character, Avatar

**Health**:
The physical vitality of the student before academic failure and game over.
_Avoid_: HP, Life, Hearts

**Stamina**:
The energy reserve consumed during rapid actions such as dashing.
_Avoid_: SP, Energy, Mana

**Experience**:
The academic progress accumulated by collecting knowledge gems dropped from defeated bugs.
_Avoid_: EXP, Score, Points

**Knowledge Gem**:
A crystallized insight dropped by defeated bugs that the student absorbs to gain Experience.
_Avoid_: ExpGem, Gem, Orb, Coin, Crystal

### Adversaries

**Bug**:
A software defect or coding error manifested as a hostile entity attacking the student.
_Avoid_: Monster, Mob, Creep, Enemy

**Syntax Error**:
A ranged bug that flees from the student while shooting projectile errors.
_Avoid_: SyntaxErr, Shooter

**Null Pointer**:
A fast melee bug that relentlessly pursues the student to inflict contact damage.
_Avoid_: NullPtr, Chaser

**Stack Overflow**:
A resilient, bouncing bug that splits into smaller sub-bugs upon defeat.
_Avoid_: StackOvfl, Splitter

**Infinite Loop**:
A durable bug with high health that relentlessly chases the student.
_Avoid_: InfLoop, Tank

**Segmentation Fault**:
An aggressive bug that telegraphs and executes high-speed charge attacks.
_Avoid_: SegFault, Charger

**Compilation Error**:
An elusive bug that maintains safe distance while firing radial projectile barrages.
_Avoid_: CompileErr

**Library Guardian**:
A mini-boss bug guarding the academic archives within the library.
_Avoid_: LibraryBoss, Mid-boss

**Professor T.H.T**:
The formidable final academic boss delivering the ultimate graduation defense trial.
_Avoid_: FinalBoss, Lecturer

### Equipment & Actions

**Weapon**:
An offensive study tool or student sustenance used to eliminate bugs.
_Avoid_: Tool, Attack, Skill

**Gear**:
A passive student essential providing ongoing stat enhancements to the student.
_Avoid_: Accessory, Artifact, Passive, Perk

**Auto-Attack**:
An offensive action that triggers periodically and automatically without direct student input.
_Avoid_: Passive Attack, Default Attack

**Active Spell**:
A specialized ability manually triggered by specific keyboard inputs.
_Avoid_: Skill, Magic, Special Move

**Upgrade**:
An academic advancement chosen upon filling the Experience threshold to enhance an item or equip a new one.
_Avoid_: Perk, Level Up Bonus, Card

### Progression & Environments

**Map**:
A discrete campus area through which the student advances sequentially.
_Avoid_: Stage, Level, Floor, Scene

**Map 1**:
The outdoor campus grounds where the student's survival journey begins.
_Avoid_: Outside, School Ground, Courtyard

**Map 2**:
The first floor lecture building where academic battles continue.
_Avoid_: Tang 1, Floor 1, Classroom

**Map 3**:
The university library archives housing intellectual trials and literature.
_Avoid_: Library, Tạ Quang Bửu

**Map 4**:
The computer laboratory where advanced hardware and software experiments reside.
_Avoid_: Lab, Phòng Lab

**Final Map**:
The high-stakes defense hall where Professor T.H.T conducts the ultimate trial.
_Avoid_: Boss Room, Exam Room, Stage 5

**Deadline**:
A scheduled surge of bugs that spawns to assault the student at timed intervals.
_Avoid_: Wave, Horde, Round, Spawn

### Objectives & Artifacts

**Key Item**:
A vital academic artifact required to activate a map's exit portal.
_Avoid_: Quest Item, Mission Item, Relic

**Admission Note**:
The key item recovered on Map 1 granting entry to the campus lecture halls.
_Avoid_: Ticket, Paper, Entrance Pass

**Lecture Notes**:
The key item gathered on Map 2 containing classroom knowledge needed to enter the library.
_Avoid_: Notebook, Homework, Sheet

**Awakened Brain**:
The key item earned on Map 3 by overcoming academic challenges to access the computer lab.
_Avoid_: Brain 100%, Smart Brain

**Source Code USB**:
The key item retrieved on Map 4 containing the compiled software necessary to enter the final defense room.
_Avoid_: Flash Drive, Memory Stick

**Portal**:
A dimensional gateway that transports the student to the next map once all prerequisites are fulfilled.
_Avoid_: Door, Teleporter, Exit, Gateway

**Academic Challenge**:
A required intellectual trial (such as pattern recognition, memory, or rapid arithmetic) within a map.
_Avoid_: Puzzle, Mini-game, Test, Quiz

**Consumable**:
Temporary sustenance (such as coffee, energy drinks, or snacks) that immediately restores vitality or grants short-term effects.
_Avoid_: Snack, Food, Potion, Power-up

### Academic Milestones & Outcomes

**Graduation**:
The ultimate victory achieved by successfully defending the thesis against Professor T.H.T in the Final Map.
_Avoid_: Win, Victory, Game Clear

**Academic Suspension**:
The state of academic failure when student Health reaches zero, terminating the current run.
_Avoid_: Game Over, Death, Defeat
